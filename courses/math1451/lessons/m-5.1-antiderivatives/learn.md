An antiderivative $F$ of $f$ satisfies $F'=f$ on an interval. Two such functions differ by a constant on that interval by the MVT. The indefinite integral $\int f(x)\,dx=F(x)+C$ represents the family of all antiderivatives. Use an initial condition such as $F(1)=4$ to select one curve.

### Reverse rules with the integrand visible
Simplify sums, radicals and reciprocals first. Integrate term by term and keep constant factors. For powers, add 1 to the exponent, then divide by the new exponent. At exponent $-1$ use $\ln|x|$ because the power rule would divide by zero. Common reverse trig pairs are $\cos x\mapsto\sin x$, $\sin x\mapsto-\cos x$, $\sec^2x\mapsto\tan x$, $\csc^2x\mapsto-\cot x$, $\sec x\tan x\mapsto\sec x$, and $\csc x\cot x\mapsto-\csc x$. Also, $1/(1+x^2)\mapsto\arctan x$ and $1/\sqrt{1-x^2}\mapsto\arcsin x$ on the appropriate intervals. For $a^x$, divide by $\ln a$ if $a>0$ and $a\ne1$.

### Use the data in sequence
With acceleration and initial velocity and position, integrate acceleration and solve the velocity constant first. Then integrate the resulting velocity and solve the position constant. Each integration has its own constant. To check any result, differentiate it and verify the given value.
