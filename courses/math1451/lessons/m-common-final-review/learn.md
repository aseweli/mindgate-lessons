The common final is cumulative. The syllabus schedules it for December 9 from 7:00 to 9:30 a.m. and lists §§2.1–5.5 for the in-class review. Chapter 1 exact algebra, trig and domain skills still support those problems. Practice without a calculator and show work.

### Work in three passes
1. **Diagnose.** Try the mixed drills below without notes. Tag each miss as a method choice, setup, theorem condition, algebra, or interpretation issue.
2. **Repair.** Redo the matching lesson and one problem with changed values. Explain aloud why your first step is valid.
3. **Simulate.** Use the timed Common Final practice set in MindGate. Reserve time to check endpoint signs, $+C$, substitutions, and units. Afterward, redo missed problems without looking at the solutions.

### Method selection
- **Limit:** try direct substitution when valid; factor, conjugate, squeeze or use a legal derivative quotient when needed.
- **Derivative:** identify the outer operation before applying product, quotient or chain rules. For an implicit curve, attach $y^\prime$ as you differentiate.
- **Derivative application:** write a domain and choose a candidate comparison, a theorem, a sign chart, or a constrained objective.
- **Integral:** for fixed bounds, evaluate exact net change; for moving bounds and a requested derivative, use the FTC and chain rule. For an indefinite integral with a nested function, look for its inner derivative and include the integration constant.
